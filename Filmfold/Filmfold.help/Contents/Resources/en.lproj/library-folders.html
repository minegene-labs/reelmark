Filmfold Help

Choose your library folders

Filmfold files movies into your Jellyfin Movies folder and episodes into your Shows folder. The first time you copy or move a file of either kind, Filmfold asks you to choose that folder.

To change a folder later, choose Filmfold › Settings, then click Choose… beside Movies or Shows.

Filmfold can only write to folders you've chosen this way. If you move or rename a library folder in Finder, Filmfold usually follows it. If it can't, an item shows “Choose a movie folder in Settings” (or a show folder), and you can choose it again.

How files are named

Each movie gets its own folder, and each show gets a folder with a folder for each season:

  • Dune - Part Two (2024) [tmdbid-693134]/Dune - Part Two (2024) [tmdbid-693134].mkv
  • Severance (2022) [tmdbid-95396]/Season 01/Severance S01E02.mkv

The TMDB number in the folder name lets Jellyfin pick the right movie or show even when titles are alike.
