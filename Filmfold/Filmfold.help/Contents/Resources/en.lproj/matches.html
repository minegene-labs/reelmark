Filmfold Help

Check and fix matches

Each row in the list shows the downloaded file, the movie or show Filmfold matched it to, and its new name in your library. The column on the right shows its status:

  • No icon: ready to file, or still being looked up.
  • Question mark: no match was found.
  • Warning triangle: something went wrong. Hold the pointer over it to see why.
  • Green checkmark: already in your library.

Choose between possible matches

When more than one movie or show could match, the Match column shows a menu. Choose the right one.

Search with a different title

Select the item and choose File › Search Again… (⌘R), or Control-click it and choose Search Again…. Type the title as it's usually written, then click Search.

Find items in a long list

Type in the search field (⌘F) to show only the items whose file name, match or new name contains those words. Press Escape to show everything again.

Look at a file

Select an item and press Space, or choose File › Quick Look (⌘Y), to preview it. To see it in Finder, choose File › Show in Finder (⇧⌘R). You can also drag items out of the list into Finder or another app; this always makes a copy.

Arrange the list

Click a column heading to sort by it, and click again to reverse the order. Control-click a heading to show or hide columns. Filmfold remembers column widths, order and hidden columns.
