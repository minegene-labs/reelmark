Filmfold Help

Get started with Filmfold

Filmfold names movies and TV episodes the way Jellyfin expects and files them into your Jellyfin library.

  1. Add downloads. Drag video files or whole download folders into the window, or choose File › Add Files… (⌘O). Filmfold finds every video inside, however deeply nested, and looks each one up on The Movie Database (TMDB).
  2. Check the matches. The New Name column shows where each file will go. See Check and fix matches.
  3. Add them to your library. Click Copy to Library (or Move to Library). The first time, Filmfold asks for your Movies or Shows folder. See Choose your library folders.

Filmfold only handles video files. Subtitles and other files stay where they are.

Other ways to add files

  • Drop videos or folders on Filmfold's Dock icon, or choose Open With › Filmfold in Finder.
  • In Finder, select files or folders, then Control-click and choose Services › Add to Filmfold.
  • Use the Add Files to Filmfold action in the Shortcuts app. See Use Filmfold with Shortcuts.
