Filmfold Help

Privacy

  • Only a title and year leave your Mac, for example “Severance 2022”, sent to TMDB. File names, folders and everything else stay on your Mac.
  • Nothing is collected: no analytics, no automatic crash reports, no update checks, and nothing about your files is logged or saved.
  • Bug reports are yours to send. Help › Report a Bug… shows exactly what it would share and opens a pre-filled report in your browser only if you choose to. File names, folders and tokens are never included.
  • Sandboxed: Filmfold can only touch the files you add and the two library folders you choose.

Your own TMDB token

Filmfold comes with its own read-only TMDB access token, so there's nothing to set up. To use your own instead, paste your TMDB API Read Access Token in Filmfold › Settings and click Save. It's stored in your Keychain. Click Remove to go back to the built-in one.
